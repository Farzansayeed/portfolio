02 — Projects

01

BhuKosh

Smart India Hackathon 2026 · Flagship

What it is

Intelligent land-record digitization and validation for the Ministry of Rural Development — scans of handwritten registers, in any Indian script, turned into validated digital records.

Why it exists

Digitization demos stop at "the AI read the text." Land records demand the harder question: can you trust it, and can you prove it? BhuKosh is built for that question.

How it works

Every extracted value stays bound to its evidence — the exact pixels it was read from, the engine run, the human decisions behind it. Business rules catch impossible data before review; human corrections teach the extractor; a truth-assurance verdict is computed from independently checkable signals, never the model's own opinion — all on a tamper-evident audit chain.

  • Python
  • JavaScript
  • PL/pgSQL
  • AI / OCR
  • OpenAPI

02

WikiExplore

What it is

A live reading portal for all of Wikipedia — 66 million articles — wrapped in an editorial experience that boots today's real featured article the moment it opens.

Why it exists

The original hackathon build was ~27 KB of hardcoded articles pretending to be a library. This is the honest version: everything live, nothing canned.

How it works

Entirely on Wikimedia APIs — featured article of the day, top-read, and on-this-day history in a single feed call. No database, no backend: a browser and the breadth of human knowledge.

  • JavaScript
  • HTML / CSS
  • Wikimedia APIs

The Living Practice

Private

What it is

A private, browser-only dashboard for daily attention and memory — plan, check-ins, focus timers from 25 to 90 minutes, and a memory deck that resurfaces on schedule.

Why it exists

Recall practice only works if it's frictionless. This one lives at a URL, opens in a second, and asks for nothing — no account, no analytics, no server.

How it works

Spaced repetition at expanding intervals — one day, three, a week, and onward — with habit check-ins, weekly goals, and JSON backup export. Everything stays in the browser.
